# Age Rating Notizen

Synapedia should use a conservative App Store age-rating stance.

## Content Categories To Disclose

The app contains:

- frequent references to psychoactive substances, alcohol, and drug interactions
- educational risk context around substance combinations
- recovery and withdrawal-oriented guide content
- medical-adjacent warning signs and emergency escalation language
- local private notes where users may enter substance-related information

The app does not contain:

- user-generated public content
- social features
- purchasing or locating substances
- explicit encouragement of illegal substance use
- personalized medical diagnosis or treatment plans
- prescription management

## Recommended Stance

Answer the App Store Connect age-rating questionnaire conservatively:

- Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References: frequent/intense
- Medical or Treatment Information: present; likely frequent depending Apple's questionnaire wording
- Mature or Suggestive Themes: use judgment based on final metadata and screenshots
- Unrestricted Web Access: no, unless external links become broad web browsing
- User-Generated Content: no

Expect a mature rating. Do not try to force a youth-oriented rating.

## Screenshot Guidance

For first external TestFlight/App Review, prefer screenshots that show:

- MixCheck risk context and red-flag language
- Wiki overview with source/fallback badge
- Sicherheit & Datenschutz screen
- Guide disclaimer

Avoid first-position screenshots that foreground private amount fields, note export, or wording that could look like planning substance use.
